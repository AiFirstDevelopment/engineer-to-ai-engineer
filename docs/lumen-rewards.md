# Lumen Rewards Program

Last updated: October 16, 2026
Owner: Tomas Reyes, Head of Customer Service

## Overview

Lumen Rewards is the loyalty program of Lantern Supply Co. It launched in April 2023 and is free
to join. Members earn points on every purchase, redeem points for rewards, and move up through
three tiers as they earn. The program is run by customer service; the points ledger lives in
Hearth and is visible to the member on the online store and to agents on the order screen.

As of October 2026 the program has 11,480 members, of whom about 9,600 are in the Ember tier,
1,650 in Flame and 230 in Beacon.

## Joining

Any customer with an email address may join on the online store, by phone, or at either
showroom. There is no fee and no minimum purchase. Each member receives a member number of the
form LR- followed by seven digits, for example LR-0048213. The member number is printed on
receipts and quoted on points emails. A customer may hold one membership; duplicate accounts
found at the same email address are merged by customer service, with the points combined.

Employees may join, and earn points on their own purchases at the discounted price actually
paid.

## Earning points

Members earn 1 point for every $1 spent on merchandise, calculated on the amount paid after
discounts and before shipping and tax. Fractions of a dollar are dropped: a $34.99 Firefly 220
earns 34 points, and a $219.99 Sentinel 1000 Pro earns 219 points. Gift card purchases earn no
points; purchases paid with a gift card earn points as usual.

Points post 48 hours after the order is delivered, or 48 hours after a showroom purchase. The
delay exists so that an order cancelled before delivery never shows points that then have to be
removed. Points from a showroom purchase post only if the member number was given at the
register; receipts can be added to an account within 30 days by calling customer service.

Points also come from:

- Writing a product review on the online store: 25 points per review, up to 100 points a year.
- The birthday reward, described below.
- Occasional double-points events, announced by email; the last was the week of
  September 14, 2026, which was double points on the Sentinel line.

Points do not come from shipping charges, tax, repair fees, gift cards, or purchases from other
retailers.

## Redeeming points

Every 500 points can be exchanged for a $25 reward. Rewards are issued as a code that can be
applied to any order on the online store or quoted at a showroom register. A reward code is valid
for 90 days from issue. Points are redeemed in multiples of 500; a member with 1,200 points can
take two rewards and keep 200 points.

A reward can be applied to any merchandise, including fuel, parts and Last Light clearance items,
but not to gift cards or shipping charges. One reward code may be used per order. The reward
reduces the amount paid and therefore the points earned on that order.

## Tiers

Tiers are set by the points earned in a calendar year, January 1 to December 31, counting points
from purchases and reviews but not the birthday reward. A member who reaches a tier keeps it for
the rest of that year and all of the following year, then is re-qualified on the new year's
earnings.

| Tier   | Points in a calendar year | Perks                                           |
|--------|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Ember  | 0 to 999                  | Points, rewards, birthday reward, member emails |
| Flame  | 1,000 to 2,499            | Ember perks, plus free expedited upgrades       |
| Beacon | 2,500 and up              | Flame perks, early Second Light access, a free repair |

### Flame

Flame members may upgrade any standard shipping order to expedited at no charge by choosing the
upgrade at checkout. The upgrade applies to the shipping service only; it does not waive the
standard shipping charge on orders under the free-shipping threshold, and it does not apply to
fuel, which ships ground only.

### Beacon

Beacon members receive the Second Light refurbished sale emails 48 hours before the general list
and may buy from them during that window. They also receive one free bench repair a year at the
Repair Center, covering the labor on an out-of-warranty repair; parts are charged. The free
repair is claimed by giving the member number when the repair ticket is opened. Beacon members'
first return of each calendar year carries no restocking fee.

Every member who reaches Beacon for the first time receives a Beacon 600 globe (G-600) engraved
with their member number, by mail. There were 230 Beacon members in October 2026.

## Birthday reward

Members who have given a birth date receive a $10 reward code on the first day of their birthday
month. The code is valid for 60 days and follows the same rules as a points reward. The birthday
reward does not count toward tier qualification. A birth date can be added or changed once;
further changes go through customer service.

## Expiry

Points expire after 18 months without a purchase. The clock restarts with every purchase that
earns points, however small. Members with points due to expire receive an email 30 days before.
Expired points are not restored, except by the Head of Customer Service in the case of a member
who was prevented from buying by a company error, such as an order wrongly cancelled.

Reward codes expire on their own schedule (90 days for a points reward, 60 days for a birthday
reward) and are not affected by the 18-month rule.

## Returns and cancellations

When an order is returned for a refund, the points earned on the returned items are removed from
the account. If the points have already been redeemed, the account goes negative and future
points fill the gap before any new reward can be taken. A reward code applied to an order that
is then fully returned is reissued with a fresh 90-day validity. Restocking fees and the prepaid
label fee do not earn or cost points.

A cancelled order earns nothing, since points post only after delivery.

## Questions agents are asked

A member says points are missing from a showroom purchase. Ask for the receipt; the member
number was probably not given at the register. Add the receipt in Hearth if it is within 30 days.

A member wants to combine points with a spouse. Memberships are individual and cannot be
combined, but a reward code can be used on anyone's order.

A member asks whether points earn on a repair. They do not; repair fees are not merchandise.

A member reached 1,000 points in December and asks when Flame starts. At once, and it lasts
through the end of the following year.

A member's reward code was lost. Agents can look it up on the account and resend it; the
expiry date does not change.
