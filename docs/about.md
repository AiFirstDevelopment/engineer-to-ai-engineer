# About Lantern Supply Co.

Last updated: October 1, 2026
Owner: Priya Whitcomb-Nair

Lantern Supply Co. sells camping lanterns, oil lamps, fuel and parts from two showrooms in
Maryland and from the online store at lanternsupply.example. We make some of what we sell, we
repair everything we sell, and we have been doing both since 1987. This page is the short version
of who we are, for new employees and for customers who ask.

## The story

Marjorie and Dale Whitcomb opened the first store in 1987 in a former hardware shop at 412 East
Patrick Street in Frederick, Maryland, the same building we occupy today. Dale had repaired
pressure lanterns for the Boy Scouts for years and could not find a shop that would sell a decent
oil lamp or a spare mantle; Marjorie ran the books and the counter. The first product with our
name on it was the Heritage No. 7 brass oil lamp, which Dale designed and had made by a brass
spinner in Baltimore, and which we still sell with a 10-year warranty.

For the first ten years the business was lamps, wicks, fuel and repairs. In 2003 the company
assembled its first Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern in a rented bay in Hagerstown, and in 2009 it
sold its first LED lantern, the Firefly 100. The online store opened in 2011. The Annapolis
showroom opened in May 2014, and in 2016 the warehouse and Repair Center moved into the current
building at 1180 Downsville Pike in Hagerstown, with the loading dock at Dock B that every return
and repair comes through.

Priya Whitcomb-Nair, Marjorie and Dale's daughter, joined the company in 2010 to run the online
store and became CEO in 2019. Marjorie and Dale retired the same year and still visit the
Frederick showroom most Saturdays. The company remains privately held and family owned.

## Where we are

The flagship showroom and headquarters are at 412 East Patrick Street, Frederick, MD 21701. The
second showroom is at 77 Compromise Street, Annapolis, MD 21401. Both showrooms are open Monday
through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern and closed on Sundays.

The warehouse and the Repair Center share one building at 1180 Downsville Pike, Dock B,
Hagerstown, MD 21740. Every online order ships from Hagerstown, every return is received there,
and every repair is done there. The building is not open to the public.

## What we believe

A lantern should last. Everything we sell is chosen or built to be repaired, and the Repair
Center will fix an out-of-warranty lantern at a fair price rather than tell you to buy a new one.
A policy should be plain. Our returns, shipping and warranty pages say what we do in numbers,
and the customer service team is allowed to say yes. A customer should be able to reach a person.
The phone is answered by someone in Frederick, Monday through Friday 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM and
Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern.

## Lights for Scouts

Since 2019 we have donated 2,400 lanterns to scout troops across Maryland through Lights for
Scouts. Each spring, troops apply through their council and receive Firefly 220 lanterns for
their summer camps, along with a repair clinic run by the Repair Center. Dale Whitcomb, who
started repairing scout lanterns before the company existed, still attends the Frederick clinic.
Customers can add a lantern to the program at checkout for $25, and the company matches every
donated lantern one for one.

## Second Light

Lanterns that come back as returns or trade-ins and pass a bench inspection are sold as Second
Light refurbished items, at a discount, with a 1-year warranty. Second Light items are sold at
both showrooms and, a few times a year, online. The program keeps good lanterns out of landfills
and gives a scout troop or a student a Sentinel at a Firefly price.

## The company

Lantern Supply Co. is a Maryland corporation, privately held and family owned. The fiscal year
runs from February 1 to January 31. The company has no outside investors and no debt beyond the
mortgage on the Hagerstown building.
