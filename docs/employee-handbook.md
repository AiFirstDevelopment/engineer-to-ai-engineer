# Employee Handbook (excerpt)

Last updated: September 8, 2026
Owner: Beth Carrow, People Operations Manager

This excerpt covers the sections of the Lantern Supply Co. employee handbook that new hires ask
about most: hours, pay, time off, benefits, the employee discount, dress code, expenses, fuel
safety on the showroom floor, customer service standards and conduct. The full handbook is on the
shared drive under People Operations and in the binder at the front desk of each showroom. Where
this excerpt and the full handbook disagree, the full handbook governs. Questions go to Beth
Carrow, People Operations Manager, or to your manager.

## Welcome

Lantern Supply Co. was founded in 1987 by Marjorie and Dale Whitcomb in Frederick, Maryland, and
is still owned and run by the family. Priya Whitcomb-Nair, the founders' daughter, has been chief
executive since 2019. As of October 2026 there are 63 of us across the Frederick headquarters and
showroom, the Annapolis showroom and the Hagerstown warehouse and Repair Center. We are a small
company that sells a small range of things well. Most of the people who buy from us are repeat
customers, and most of them know a great deal about lanterns. Treat them that way.

## Hours and schedules

### Showrooms

Both showrooms, Frederick (412 East Patrick Street) and Annapolis (77 Compromise Street), are
open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern, and closed on Sundays. Showroom staff
arrive 30 minutes before opening to count the drawer, check display stock and turn on the
demonstration lanterns, and stay up to 30 minutes after closing to shut down. Those 30 minutes are
paid time and should be on your timesheet.

### Customer support

Phone and email support runs Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern, and Saturday
9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern. Support is staffed from the Frederick office. Saturday coverage rotates
through the support team on a schedule Tomas Reyes posts four weeks ahead.

### Warehouse and Repair Center

The Hagerstown warehouse (1180 Downsville Pike) runs Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM
Eastern, with a Saturday shift of 8:00 AM to noon from November 1 through December 23 for the
holiday season. Carrier pickups are at 3:00 PM, which is why orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern
on a business day ship the same day.

### Schedules and timekeeping

Hourly employees clock in and out in Deputy, the scheduling app. Schedules are posted two weeks in
advance. Swaps are fine if both people agree and the shift lead approves them in Deputy before the
shift starts. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager; Maryland law requires us to pay
it whether or not it was approved, but unapproved overtime is a conduct issue.

## Pay

Everyone is paid twice a month, on the 15th and on the last day of the month. When a pay date falls
on a weekend or a bank holiday, pay is deposited the business day before. Pay is by direct deposit;
paper checks are available on request from Harold Nkemelu's office and are mailed, not held.

Pay periods run from the 1st through the 15th and from the 16th through the end of the month.
Hourly employees are paid for the period that ended on the pay date; salaried employees are paid
current. Pay stubs are in Gusto. Report a pay error to People Operations within 30 days.

Annual pay reviews happen in February, after the fiscal year closes on January 31. Changes take
effect with the pay period beginning March 1.

## Time off

### Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees earn 15 days of PTO in each of their first three years and 20 days a year once
they have completed three years of service. PTO accrues monthly, in twelve equal parts, on the last
day of each month. New hires may use PTO once they have completed 60 days. Part-time employees
earn PTO in proportion to scheduled hours.

Up to 5 days of unused PTO carry over into the next calendar year; the rest is forfeited on
December 31. Unused PTO is paid out when you leave, as Maryland requires when a written policy
says so, and this policy says so.

Request PTO in Deputy at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than two days. Showroom
requests for the week of Thanksgiving and the two weeks before Christmas are approved on a
first-come basis and are limited to two people per showroom at a time.

### Sick and safe leave

Under the Maryland Healthy Working Families Act, every employee earns sick and safe leave at one
hour for every 30 hours worked, up to 40 hours (five days) a year. Lantern Supply Co. provides 7
days (56 hours) a year, which is more than the law requires, and front-loads it on January 1 for
everyone employed on that date. Sick and safe leave may be used for your own illness or medical
care, for a family member's, or for the reasons the Act lists around domestic violence. Up to 7
days of unused sick and safe leave carry over; the balance you can hold at any time is capped at
14 days. Sick leave is not paid out when you leave.

### Holidays

The company is closed, and the showrooms and support lines do not operate, on six holidays:

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day

Full-time employees are paid for these days. When a holiday falls on a Sunday it is observed on
Monday; when it falls on a Saturday the showrooms close that Saturday. The warehouse observes the
same six days. We are open on the day after Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve, closing at 3:00 PM
on Christmas Eve.

### Other leave

Jury duty is paid for up to 10 days a year. Bereavement leave is 3 paid days for an immediate
family member. Parental leave is 6 weeks paid for a birth or adoption after one year of service,
which may be combined with PTO and with leave under federal and Maryland law. Talk to Beth Carrow
before any leave longer than two weeks so paperwork is in place.

## Benefits

### Health insurance

Medical, dental and vision coverage is through the CareFirst Keystone Select plan. Eligibility
begins on the first day of the month after 30 days of full-time employment. The company pays 75% of
the employee premium and 50% of the premium for dependents. Open enrollment runs November 1
through November 20 each year, for coverage starting January 1. Outside open enrollment you can
change your election only after a qualifying life event, within 30 days of the event.

### Retirement

The 401(k) plan is administered by Guideline. You may enroll after 90 days of employment, and the
company matches 100% of your contributions up to 4% of pay. The match vests immediately. Enrollment
is through the Guideline link in Gusto; contributions start with the next pay period.

### Other benefits

Full-time employees receive a company-paid life insurance policy of $25,000, a pretax commuter
benefit of up to $150 a month, and an annual stipend of $200 for work boots or safety footwear for
warehouse and repair staff. The company pays for the ServSafe-style fuel handling certification
required for warehouse staff and renews it every three years.

## Employee discount

Every employee, full-time or part-time, gets 30% off the retail price of anything we sell, in the
showrooms or on the online store, and 50% off Second Light refurbished items. The discount applies
to merchandise, not to shipping, repairs or gift cards. Employees may spend up to $2,000 a year at
the discounted price; purchases past the cap are at retail. The cap resets on January 1.

The discount is for you and the people in your household. Buying discounted items to resell, or
for friends outside your household, is a conduct violation and ends the discount. Use the
discount code on your employee ID card online, or give your employee number at the register. Your
discounted purchases still earn Lumen Rewards points on the amount you actually paid.

## Dress code

Showroom and support staff wear the lantern green polo (we provide three) with your name tag,
with dark trousers, jeans without holes, or a knee-length skirt, and closed-toe shoes. Warehouse
and repair staff wear the grey Lantern Supply Co. work shirt, long trousers and steel-toe or
composite-toe boots; the boot stipend above covers them. Hats are fine in the warehouse, not in
the showrooms. Replacement polos and name tags come from Beth Carrow; a replacement name tag
takes about a week.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims in Expensify within 30 days of the expense. Receipts are required for any
item over $25. Mileage for driving your own car on company business, for example between
showrooms or to the Hagerstown warehouse, is paid at the IRS standard rate in effect that year.
Meals are reimbursed at actual cost up to $20 for lunch and $35 for dinner when you are away from
your home location over a mealtime. Your manager approves claims under $250; Harold Nkemelu
approves claims of $250 and up. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll.

## Fuel safety in the showrooms

Both showrooms stock fuel for sale, but fuel on a showroom floor is limited to 2 quarts of display
stock in total, across Clearburn white gas, Clearburn lamp oil and kerosene. Everything else stays
in the locked fuel cabinet in the back room, which holds no more than the quantity on the
cabinet's placard. There is no fuel room at either showroom; the only fuel room in the company is
at the Hagerstown warehouse, and bulk fuel is never stored at a showroom overnight outside the
cabinet.

Demonstration lanterns on the floor run on Clearburn white gas or lamp oil as the product
requires. Never fuel a demonstration lantern on the sales floor; take it to the back-room fueling
tray. Never fuel a hot lantern; let it cool for 10 minutes. Spills are cleaned at once with the
absorbent pads in the cabinet, and the pads go in the red bin, not the trash.

Fire extinguishers at every location are checked monthly by Denise Okafor, the Warehouse
Manager, or by a person she designates in writing, and the check is recorded on the tag. If a tag
is more than 31 days old, tell your manager that day. Every employee is shown how to use an
extinguisher in their first week. A showroom never has fewer than two working extinguishers on
the floor.

Fuel sold in a showroom goes out in the customer's hands; we never ship fuel from a showroom.
Fuel ordered online ships by ground only from Hagerstown, never to a PO box and never to Alaska,
Hawaii or Canada. If a customer asks you to make an exception, the answer is no.

## Customer service standards

Every email to help@lanternsupply.example is answered within 1 business day, and most are answered
the same day. A phone caller should wait less than 3 minutes; if the queue is longer than that,
the shift lead pulls in a second person. Chat on the online store follows the same 3-minute
standard during support hours and shows the next-day email promise outside them.

Know the policies before you quote them. The return window is 40 days from the delivery date,
the standard warranty is 2 years, and the full details are in the returns policy, the shipping
policy, the warranty policy and the customer service playbook. Any refund, credit or goodwill over
$500 needs approval from Tomas Reyes, Head of Customer Service, and anything over $2,000 needs
Priya Whitcomb-Nair's. Do not promise what you cannot approve.

## Breaks and meal periods

Anyone working more than 6 consecutive hours takes an unpaid meal break of at least 30 minutes,
as Maryland's Healthy Retail Employee Act requires for retail staff, and a paid 15-minute break
for every 4 hours worked. Showroom breaks are staggered so that at least two people are on the
floor at all times; the shift lead sets the order each morning. Meal breaks are clocked out in
Deputy. Warehouse breaks follow the bell at 9:30 AM, noon and 2:15 PM. Nobody eats at the repair
bench or near the fuel cabinet.

## Training

Every new hire spends their first week in the Frederick showroom, whatever their role, learning the
products by using them: lighting a Northstar, trimming a wick on a Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern,
charging and running down a Firefly 220R, and taking a Sentinel 1000 apart as far as the light
panels. Showroom and support staff then complete the product knowledge course in Trainual, in
nine modules, within 30 days, and pass a 40-question check at 80% or better. The course is
refreshed each February when the catalog changes.

Warehouse and repair staff complete fuel handling certification before they handle fuel
unsupervised, usually in the second week, and repeat it every three years. Forklift operators in
Hagerstown are certified by Denise Okafor and recertified every three years as OSHA requires.
Repair technicians train for twelve weeks under Walt Brannigan before they sign off a repair on
their own, starting with LED products and ending with the Northstar.

Anyone who wants to learn another part of the business can ask for a shadow day: a day in the
warehouse, on the bench, or on the support phones. Managers approve one shadow day a quarter per
person when staffing allows.

## Performance reviews

Reviews happen once a year, in January, before the pay reviews in February, with a short check-in
in July. Your manager writes the review in Gusto, you add your own comments, and you meet for
about 45 minutes. Reviews cover the work itself, how you treat customers and colleagues, and
safety. Nobody should be surprised by anything in a review; if a concern is serious enough to go in
writing in January, it was raised in person earlier.

Showroom associates are also measured on the mystery-shop visits a contractor makes to each
showroom four times a year. The report comes to the showroom manager and is shared with the team;
it is a coaching tool, not a disciplinary one, unless it shows a safety or honesty problem.

## Keys, alarms and cash

Managers, assistant managers and shift leads hold keys and alarm codes. Codes are personal and
are changed when someone leaves. The showroom safe is opened only by a key holder, and cash over
$500 in a register drawer is dropped to the safe. Deposits go to the bank on Tuesday and Friday
mornings, and two people go. A lost key is reported the same day to Beth Carrow and the locks are
changed at the company's expense the first time.

## Conduct

We expect honesty with customers and with each other, care with fuel and with other people's
property, and plain courtesy. The following are grounds for discipline up to and including
dismissal: theft, including misuse of the employee discount; falsifying a timesheet or expense
claim; coming to work impaired; harassment or discrimination of any kind; ignoring a fuel safety
rule; and sharing customer information outside the company. Lantern Supply Co. is an equal
opportunity employer and complies with federal and Maryland law in hiring, pay and promotion.

Concerns about conduct, including harassment, go to your manager, to Beth Carrow, or, if either
is part of the concern, to Priya Whitcomb-Nair directly. Retaliation against anyone who raises a
concern in good faith is itself a conduct violation.

### Personal phones and social media

Phones stay off the sales floor and the bench except on breaks. You may say online that you work
here; you may not speak for the company, post customer information, or post photographs of the
repair bench, the warehouse or the back rooms without permission from Ana Lindqvist, who runs the
company's online presence as well as the online store.

### Weather closures

When Frederick County or Anne Arundel County public schools close for weather, the showroom in
that county opens two hours late; when they close early, the showroom closes early. A full-day
closure is decided by 7:00 AM by Priya Whitcomb-Nair and posted in the staff Slack channel and on
the store's website. Hourly employees are paid for scheduled hours lost to a full-day weather
closure, up to two days a year.

## Acknowledgement

Every employee signs an acknowledgement in Gusto that they have received this handbook, within
their first week and again whenever a new edition is issued. Signing means you have read it and
will ask about anything you do not understand; it does not create an employment contract.
Employment at Lantern Supply Co. is at will. This edition of the handbook was issued on
September 8, 2026 and replaces the edition of March 2, 2025.
