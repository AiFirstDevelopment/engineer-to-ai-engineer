# Showrooms

Last updated: August 24, 2026
Owner: Priya Whitcomb-Nair, Chief Executive

Lantern Supply Co. has two showrooms, both in Maryland: the flagship in Frederick, where the
company started in 1987, and the Annapolis showroom, opened in May 2014. Both are open Monday
through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern, and closed on Sundays and on the six company
holidays. Both take in-store returns with the packing slip or order number, both accept repair
drop-offs, and both stock fuel for pickup. Only Frederick offers curbside pickup.

## Frederick showroom

### Address and hours

412 East Patrick Street, Frederick, MD 21701. Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Eastern. Closed Sundays. The showroom shares the building with the company headquarters and the
customer support office, which occupy the second floor; the showroom is the whole ground floor,
about 3,200 square feet.

The showroom manager is Marcus Delacroix, who has been with the company since 2008. The assistant
manager is Yolanda Pruitt.

### Parking and directions

Park in the East Patrick Street garage, one block east of the showroom. Bring your ticket to the
register for validation; we cover up to 2 hours. Street parking on East Patrick is metered and
limited to one hour, so the garage is the better choice for anyone browsing or dropping off a
repair.

From I-70, take exit 56 for Route 144 (Patrick Street) west and continue about a mile and a half
into downtown; the showroom is on the right, past the Carroll Creek bridge. From I-270, follow
Route 15 north to the Rosemont Avenue exit, then East Patrick Street east. The MARC Brunswick line
stops at Frederick station, a six-minute walk away.

### Services

Curbside pickup: place an order on the online store and choose Curbside pickup, Frederick. It is
free, and the order is ready within 2 hours during showroom hours; orders placed within 2 hours of
closing are ready at opening the next day. Park in the two marked bays in front of the showroom
and call (301) 555-0147, option 3, and someone brings the order out. Fuel can be picked up
curbside, which is the only way to get fuel without entering a showroom.

Repair drop-off: bring the lantern to the register with your order number if you have it. We
open a repair ticket on the spot, give you the RT- number, and the lantern goes to the Hagerstown
Repair Center on the next van, which runs from Frederick every Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
Fuel lanterns must be empty when dropped off; we have a drain tray in the back room if yours is
not.

Wick-trimming clinic: the first Saturday of each month at 10:00 AM, free, no sign-up needed.
Walt Brannigan or one of the repair technicians shows how to trim and set a flat wick, clean a
burner and replace a mantle, using the Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern, the Heritage No. 7 and the
Northstar. Bring your own lantern (empty) if you want help with it. The clinic runs about 45
minutes.

### What is stocked

The Frederick showroom carries the full catalog: every current LED lantern, the Northstar
Dual-Fuel, the Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern in all three colors, the Heritage No. 7, the Porchlight
Solar, and all fuel, mantles, wicks, globes and chimneys. It is the only showroom that stocks
Second Light refurbished items, on the back wall near the repair counter. Display stock of fuel on
the floor is limited to 2 quarts, as everywhere; the rest is in the locked cabinet and brought out
on request.

### Accessibility

Level entry from the sidewalk, a 36-inch-wide door, aisles at least 40 inches wide, and an
accessible restroom at the rear. The register has a lowered counter section. A service animal is
welcome. The East Patrick Street garage has accessible spaces on the ground level with an elevator.

## Annapolis showroom

### Address and hours

77 Compromise Street, Annapolis, MD 21401, at the foot of Main Street near the City Dock. Opened
May 2014. Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern. Closed Sundays. About 1,400 square
feet on one floor.

The showroom manager is Dana Okonkwo-Reilly, who opened the store in 2014. There is no assistant
manager; the senior sales associate, Ben Takahashi, is the shift lead when Dana is off.

### Parking and directions

Street parking on Compromise Street and around the City Dock is metered, two hours maximum, and
scarce on summer weekends. The Hillman Garage on Duke of Gloucester Street, a four-minute walk, is
the fallback; we do not validate there. The Circulator trolley stops at the City Dock.

From Route 50, take exit 24 for Rowe Boulevard, continue to College Avenue, then King George
Street and Randall Street to the dock; Compromise Street runs along the water. Boaters tying up at
the City Dock are a short walk away, and many of our Annapolis customers arrive that way.

### Services

Repair drop-off: bring the lantern to the register. We open the repair ticket and hold the lantern
in the back room. Annapolis repairs are shipped to the Hagerstown Repair Center once a week, on
Tuesdays, so an Annapolis drop-off takes about a week longer than one in Frederick. Fuel lanterns
must be empty; Annapolis has no drain tray, so please empty the lantern at home first.

There is no curbside pickup at Annapolis, and no wick-trimming clinic. Online orders cannot be
collected here. Customers who want to collect an order should choose Curbside pickup, Frederick, or
have it shipped.

In-store returns are accepted with the packing slip or the order number, and no RA number is
needed.

### What is stocked

The Annapolis showroom leans toward what people on and around the water buy: the Sentinel 1000
and Sentinel 1000 Pro, the Firefly 220 and Firefly 220R, the Beacon 600, the Hagerstown Kerosene
Lantern in galvanized and black, the Heritage No. 7 and the Porchlight Solar. It does not stock the
Northstar Dual-Fuel on the floor, though it can be ordered for shipping, and it carries Clearburn
lamp oil and kerosene but not Clearburn white gas. Mantles, wicks and chimneys are stocked; globes
are ordered. No Second Light items.

### Accessibility

There is one step at the Compromise Street entrance; a portable ramp is kept inside and staff will
set it out on request, so please knock or call (301) 555-0147, option 4. The aisles are 36 inches
wide. The restroom is not accessible; the nearest public accessible restroom is at the Harbormaster
building at the City Dock.

## Contact

Both showrooms are reached through the main number, (301) 555-0147: option 3 for Frederick, option
4 for Annapolis. Email help@lanternsupply.example reaches customer support, which can pass a
message to either showroom the same day.
