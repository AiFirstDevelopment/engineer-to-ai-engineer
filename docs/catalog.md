## How to read a model number

Every product we make carries a model number that starts with LS-. For LED lanterns the number
that follows is the lumen class: an LS-220 is a 220-lumen lantern, an LS-600 is a 600-lumen
lantern, an LS-1000 is a 1,000-lumen lantern. A P suffix means Pro, the larger version of the
same lantern. An R suffix means rechargeable: the lantern has a built-in battery and charges over
USB-C instead of taking replaceable cells. Fuel lanterns and lamps use letters instead of a lumen
class (ND for Northstar Dual-Fuel, HK for Hagerstown Kerosene, H for Heritage), because the light
output of a flame depends on the fuel and the mantle or wick.

Parts have their own prefixes: M for mantles, G for globes, W for wicks, CH for chimneys, C for
cables and B for battery packs. A part number is printed inside the base of every lantern so a
customer can read it off without looking anything up.

## LED lanterns

Every LED lantern in this catalogue uses warm white LEDs at 3,000 K, rated for 50,000 hours,
which is longer than the lantern will last; the LEDs are not a replaceable part. Lumen figures
are measured at the globe on high with fresh batteries or a full charge, and fall as the batteries
drain. Runtimes on low are to the point where the output drops below 10 percent of its rating.

## Ordering and availability

Every item in this catalogue is stocked at the Hagerstown warehouse and ships from there. The
showrooms carry the full LED line, the Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern and the Heritage No. 7 on the
floor; the Northstar and the Sentinel 1000 Pro are kept in the back room and brought out on
request. An item shown as backordered on the online store ships in the order the orders were
placed, and the order confirmation gives the expected ship date. We do not charge a card until
the item ships.

## Price Promise

If you find a lower price on the same product from an authorized retailer within 14 days of your
purchase from us, send the link or the receipt to help@lanternsupply.example and we will refund
the difference. The product must be new, the same model number, and in stock at the other
retailer at the time you write. Marketplace sellers, auctions and clearance pricing are excluded.
