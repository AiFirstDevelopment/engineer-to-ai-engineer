# Returns Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026
Owner: Tomas Reyes, Head of Customer Service

## Purpose

This document is the returns policy of Lantern Supply Co. as it applies to orders placed on the
online store, by phone, and in either showroom. Customer service agents, showroom staff and the
Hagerstown warehouse follow it as written. The customer-facing summary on lanternsupply.example
is derived from this document and must not promise more than it does. Questions about an
exception go to the shift lead first, then to Tomas Reyes.

## The return window

A customer may return an eligible item within 40 days of the delivery date. The window is counted
from the date the carrier recorded delivery, not from the date the order was placed and not from
the date it shipped. For showroom purchases, the delivery date is the purchase date on the
receipt. For curbside pickup orders, it is the date the order was handed over at the Frederick
showroom.

Day one is the day after delivery. A return is on time if the customer starts it (requests an RA
number online or by phone, or brings the item to a showroom) on or before day 40. The item itself
may arrive at the warehouse after day 40 as long as the RA number was issued on time and the
package is postmarked within 7 days of the RA number being issued.

### How the window is counted

Since Hearth 4.1, released May 19, 2026, the online store counts the 40 days from the delivery
date as recorded by the carrier. Before 4.1 the store counted the window from the ship date, which
cost customers two to six days and produced a steady stream of complaints about packages that
spent a week in transit. Orders placed before May 19, 2026 that are still open for any reason
are handled under the delivery-date rule as well; agents do not need to look up which rule was in
force.

### Worked examples

Order LS-2026-004871 shipped on June 2, 2026 and was delivered on June 5, 2026. Day one is June 6.
The last day to start a return is July 15, 2026.

Order LS-2026-011302 was delivered on November 14, 2026. Under the ordinary rule the window
would close on December 24, 2026. Because the order was delivered between November 1 and
December 24, the holiday extension applies and the customer has until January 31, 2027.

Order LS-2026-009140 was picked up curbside at Frederick on September 9, 2026. The last day to
start a return is October 19, 2026.

## What can be returned

An item is eligible for return when all of the following are true:

- It is unused. A lantern that has been switched on to check that it works is still unused. A
  lantern that has been taken on a trip is not.
- It is in its original packaging with all accessories, inserts, manuals and the warranty card.
- It was purchased from Lantern Supply Co. directly: the online store, by phone, or at the
  Frederick or Annapolis showroom. Items bought from another retailer go back to that retailer.
- It is not on the final-sale list below.

### Fuel put in means no return

Any fuel lantern or oil lamp that has had fuel put in it cannot be returned for a refund or an
exchange, regardless of how little fuel was used or whether it was lit. This covers the Northstar
Dual-Fuel (LS-ND2), the Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern (LS-HK12) and the Heritage No. 7 (LS-H7).
Once fuel has been in the tank or font the item is a hazardous-materials shipment and the
warehouse cannot restock it. A fueled lantern with a defect is handled under the warranty policy,
not the returns policy. Agents should ask directly whether fuel has been put in before issuing an
RA number for any of these three models.

### Final sale, no returns

The following are sold as final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

- Clearance items marked "Last Light" on the product page, the price tag or the receipt.
- Custom-engraved items, including engraved Heritage No. 7 lamps and engraved gift sets.
- Gift cards, physical and electronic.
- Opened fuel: Clearburn White Gas (LS-FUEL1), Clearburn Lamp Oil (LS-OIL1) and kerosene.
- Mantles (M-21), wicks (W-78), chimneys (CH-7), globes and other consumables once the
  packaging has been opened.

Unopened fuel and unopened consumables may be returned under the ordinary rules, but fuel cannot
be mailed back; it must be brought to a showroom.

## Restocking fee

A restocking fee of 12% of the item price applies to any opened, non-defective item. "Opened"
means the factory seal or shrink wrap has been broken, or the item has been removed from its
inner packaging. An item returned in an unopened, sealed box carries no fee.

The fee is waived when:

- The return is an exchange for another item of equal or greater value.
- The item is defective, as confirmed by customer service or the Repair Center.
- The wrong item was shipped, or the item does not match its product page.
- The customer is a Beacon-tier Lumen Rewards member and the return is their first of the
  calendar year.

The fee is calculated on the price paid after discounts and before tax and shipping. On a Beacon
600 (LS-600) bought at $79.99, the fee is $9.60. On a Sentinel 1000 Pro (LS-1000P) at $219.99,
the fee is $26.40. The fee is never charged on a showroom return made within 7 days of purchase
with the receipt.

## Return shipping

The customer pays return shipping unless the item is defective or the wrong item was shipped.
Customers may use any carrier they like and are responsible for the package until it arrives
at the Hagerstown warehouse; the Customer Service team recommends tracking and insurance on
anything over $100.

A prepaid return label is offered on every mailed return for a flat $8.50, deducted from the
refund. The label is a UPS Ground label addressed to the warehouse and is good for 14 days from
the day it is issued. One label covers one package; a return of more than one package needs a
label per package, each at $8.50.

When the item is defective or the wrong item was shipped, the prepaid label is free and no
restocking fee applies.

## Return authorization numbers

Every mailed return needs a return authorization number before it ships. RA numbers have the form
RA- followed by six digits, for example RA-118204. The number is printed on the packing slip
that comes with the prepaid label and must also be written on the outside of the box.

Customers obtain an RA number by:

- Using "Start a return" on the online store, which issues the number immediately for any
  eligible order and emails it with a packing slip.
- Calling (301) 555-0147 during support hours. The agent creates the return in Hearth and reads
  the number back.

A package that arrives at the warehouse without an RA number is held for 10 business days while
the warehouse tries to match it to an order. If it cannot be matched it is returned to the sender
at the sender's cost. The RA number is valid for 30 days from issue.

## Refunds

Refunds go to the original payment method. The warehouse inspects each return within 2 business
days of arrival and the refund is issued within 7 business days of the return arriving at the
Hagerstown warehouse. Card refunds typically appear on the customer's statement 3 to 5 business
days after they are issued; this is the card issuer's timing and the company cannot speed it up.

The refund covers the item price paid, less any restocking fee and less the $8.50 prepaid label
fee if one was used. Original outbound shipping charges are refunded only when the item was
defective or the wrong item was shipped.

If the original payment method is a card that has since been closed, the refund is issued as a
store credit to the customer's Lumen Rewards account, or as a check mailed within 14 business
days if the customer has no account.

Gift recipients returning an item without the original payment details receive store credit for
the item's price on the gift receipt.

## In-store returns

Both showrooms accept returns of items bought online, by phone, or in either showroom. The
customer should bring the packing slip or the order number and the payment card used. Showroom
staff process the return in Hearth on the spot; the refund timing is the same 7 business days.
Items returned in store are held in the showroom's returns cage and shipped to Hagerstown on the
weekly Tuesday transfer.

Showrooms may accept unopened fuel and unopened consumables, which cannot be mailed. Showrooms do
not accept items that have had fuel put in them, for the same reason the warehouse does not.

## Exchanges

An exchange is processed as a return and a new order. The replacement ships as soon as the
exchange is created in Hearth, before the returned item arrives, when the replacement is the same
model in a different color or the price difference is under $50. For any other exchange the
replacement ships after the return is received. No restocking fee is charged on an exchange.

## Holiday extension

Orders delivered between November 1 and December 24 may be returned until January 31 of the
following year. All other terms of this policy still apply: the item must be unused, in its
packaging, not final sale, and fuel-free. The extension applies automatically in Hearth; agents do
not need to adjust the window by hand.

## Approvals: the Three Lights rule

Any refund, credit or goodwill gesture over $500 on a single order needs the approval of Tomas
Reyes before it is issued. Anything over $2,000 needs the approval of Priya Whitcomb-Nair. Agents
may issue goodwill credits of up to $25 per incident on their own authority. The approval is
recorded as a note on the order in Hearth with the approver's name and the date. The rule takes
its name from the three lights on the old warehouse sign and is explained in full in the Customer
Service Playbook.

## Common situations

A customer wants to return a lantern after 40 days. The answer is no, unless the holiday extension
applies. Agents may offer to check the item under warranty if there is a defect.

A customer bought a Firefly 220R (LS-220R) and meant to buy the Firefly 220 (LS-220). This is a
simple exchange; no restocking fee, and the replacement ships at once because the price
difference is under $50.

A customer has lit their new Northstar once in the backyard and does not like the sound of the
pressurized tank. The item cannot be returned; fuel has been put in it. The agent should explain
why and note that the warranty still covers any defect.

A customer's return arrived at the warehouse damaged in transit without insurance. The warehouse
photographs the damage and customer service offers a partial refund at the shift lead's
discretion; the customer is responsible for a return package until it arrives.

## Record keeping

Every return is a record in Hearth tied to the order number and the RA number. The warehouse
records the arrival date, the inspection result and the restocked location. Returns data is
reviewed monthly by Tomas Reyes and Denise Okafor, and the return rate by model is reported to
Priya Whitcomb-Nair at the quarterly review.
