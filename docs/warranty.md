# Warranty Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026
Owner: Walt Brannigan, Head of Repairs

## What the warranty promises

Lantern Supply Co. warrants that every lantern and lamp it sells is free of defects in materials
and workmanship for the warranty period listed below, counted from the date of purchase. If a
product fails because of such a defect within the period, the company will repair it or replace
it, at the company's choice, at no charge to the customer, and will pay shipping to and from the
Repair Center for an approved claim.

The warranty is a promise about how the product was made, not about how long it will last under
any treatment. Wear, accidents and misuse are not defects. The exclusions below spell out the
line.

The standard warranty is 2 years. The Sentinel 1000 and Sentinel 1000 Pro carry 5 years because
their housings and electronics are designed for it, and the company has the parts to honor it.
The Heritage No. 7 carries 10 years under the Heritage Promise, which dates from the lamp's
introduction in 1994 and has never been shortened.

Rechargeable batteries and battery packs, whether built in or removable, are covered for 1 year
from the date of purchase, regardless of the warranty period of the product they are in. A
battery is considered defective if it holds less than 70% of its rated capacity within the first
year under normal charging; capacity loss beyond that is wear.

Second Light refurbished items are covered for 1 year from purchase, whatever the model's new
warranty would be. The refurbished warranty is noted on the receipt.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Consumables: mantles (M-21), globes (G-600, G-ND2), wicks (W-78), chimneys (CH-7), bulbs, fuel
  caps, pump cups, O-rings and gaskets. These wear by design and are sold as parts.
- Damage from a drop, a crush, or being run over, including a cracked housing or a bent frame.
- Water intrusion beyond the product's rated IP level. A Firefly 220 (IPX4) left out in a storm
  is not a warranty case; a Sentinel 1000 (IP67) that leaks after a 30-minute dunk at one meter
  is.
- Use of an unapproved fuel. Kerosene in a Northstar, gasoline in a Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern,
  or anything other than lamp oil in a Heritage No. 7 voids the warranty for the affected parts
  and usually for the whole product.
- Modifications, including replacement parts not supplied by Lantern Supply Co., drilled
  housings and rewired switches.
- Commercial rental use. A lantern bought for a rental fleet is covered for 90 days only, and the
  customer should say so at purchase.
- Cosmetic wear: scratches, fading, tarnish on brass, and discoloration of globes from use.
- Loss or theft.

The company reserves the right to inspect a product before deciding a claim and to decline a
claim where the inspection shows one of the exclusions above.

## Making a claim

A warranty claim starts with an email to help@lanternsupply.example, or a call to (301) 555-0147,
or a visit to either showroom. The customer should provide:

- The order number (LS-2026-xxxxxx) or a copy of the showroom receipt. For a gift, the gift
  receipt or the giver's order number.
- A description of the problem.
- Photos of the product and, where the problem can be seen, of the fault. For an electrical
  fault a short video is helpful but not required.

Customer service opens the claim in Hearth and issues a warranty claim number of the form
WC- followed by six digits, for example WC-030517. The customer quotes this number in any
follow-up.

### Decision

A decision is made within 5 business days of the claim being opened. Most claims are decided from
the photos by customer service using the Repair Center's guidance; claims the photos cannot settle
are referred to the Repair Center for inspection, and the 5-day clock still applies to the
decision to inspect. The customer is told the decision by email.

The possible decisions are:

- Approved for replacement: a new unit ships, and the defective unit is either returned with a
  free prepaid label or, for items under $50, discarded by the customer.
- Approved for repair: a free prepaid label is sent and the product goes to the Repair Center.
- Approved for part: a replacement part ships and the customer fits it, with instructions. This
  is the usual outcome for a failed switch or a cracked detachable panel.
- Declined: the reason is given, with an offer of a paid repair where one is possible.

Whether a product is repaired or replaced is the company's choice, made on cost and on the
availability of parts. A product that is no longer made, such as the Beacon 400 (LS-400), is
repaired from stocked parts where possible and otherwise replaced with the nearest current model;
the Beacon 400 is replaced with a Beacon 600.

### Shipping for an approved claim

For an approved claim the company pays shipping both ways. The customer receives a prepaid UPS
Ground label addressed to:

Lantern Supply Co. Repair Center
1180 Downsville Pike, Dock B
Hagerstown, MD 21740

The product must be packed in a box with enough padding that it does not move. Fuel lanterns and
oil lamps must be completely drained and left open to air for 24 hours before packing; the
Repair Center refuses any package that smells of fuel, and the carrier may fine the sender. The
Repair Center acknowledges arrival by email the same business day.

Customers may also drop a product at either showroom. Frederick sends it to Hagerstown on the
Tuesday van; Annapolis does the same. A drop-off at a showroom still needs a WC- number.

### Turnaround

A warranty repair is completed within 10 business days of arrival at the Repair Center where the
parts are in stock, which is the case for every current model. Replacements ship within 2
business days of approval. If a repair will take longer because a part is on order, the customer
is told the expected date and may ask for a replacement instead.

## Proof of purchase

The warranty period runs from the date of purchase shown on the order or receipt. Hearth holds
every online, phone and showroom sale since 2018, so a customer who has lost the receipt can
usually be found by name, email or card. Where no record can be found, the Repair Center dates
the product from the serial number's manufacturing date and allows 6 months for time on the shelf.

## Transfer of the warranty

The warranty transfers with a gift: a recipient can claim under the giver's purchase date with a
gift receipt or the giver's order number. The warranty does not transfer with a resale; a product
bought second-hand is not covered, and the company's Second Light program is the only route to a
warranted used product.

## Registration

Registration is optional. A customer who registers a product on the online store, with the order
number and the serial number, is contacted directly if the company issues a safety notice for
that model, and does not need to produce a receipt for a claim. Registration does not extend the
warranty period.

## Out of warranty

Products outside their warranty period, and declined claims, can be repaired by the Repair Center
for a fee. The customer is quoted before any work is done and can decline the quote, in which
case the product is returned at the customer's shipping cost. The Repair Center's own manual
describes the fee schedule and the shop procedures.

## Safety notices

If a defect is found that affects safety, the company notifies registered owners directly,
publishes a notice on the website and offers a free repair or replacement regardless of warranty
status. The last such notice was in February 2021 for a batch of Beacon 400 battery springs; it
is closed.

## Relationship to the returns policy

The returns policy covers a change of mind within 40 days of delivery for an unused item. The
warranty covers a defect at any time within the warranty period, used or not. A fueled lantern
cannot be returned but can always be claimed under warranty. Agents should route a customer with
a working product who does not want it to the returns policy, and a customer with a product that
does not work to this document.
