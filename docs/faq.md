## Orders and shipping

### How long will my order take to arrive?

Orders placed before 2:00 PM Eastern on a business day leave our Hagerstown warehouse the same
day. Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days by UPS Ground or USPS. Expedited shipping takes
2 business days and overnight shipping arrives the next business day if you order by 1:00 PM
Eastern.

### Is shipping free?

Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more. Below that, standard shipping is a flat $6.95.
Expedited shipping is $14.95 and overnight is $29.95 on any order size.

### Do you ship outside the United States?

We ship to all 50 states and to Canada. Shipping to Canada is a flat $19.95 and takes 5 to 10
business days; duties and taxes are paid by the recipient on delivery. We do not ship to any
other country.

### Do you ship fuel to Canada?

No. Clearburn white gas, lamp oil and kerosene ship by ground only inside the United States, and
never to Canada, Alaska, Hawaii or a PO box. If a Canadian order contains fuel, we remove the fuel
and refund it before the rest of the order ships.

### Can I pick my order up instead?

Yes, at the Frederick showroom only. Choose curbside pickup at checkout and your order is ready
within 2 hours during store hours, Monday through Saturday 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Pickup is free.
The Annapolis showroom does not offer curbside pickup.

### My package says delivered but I don't have it. What now?

Check with neighbors and your building's office first; most packages turn up within a day. If it
has not arrived 10 business days after the expected delivery date, write to us with the order
number and we will ship a replacement before the carrier claim is settled.

### My order arrived damaged. Do I have to send it back?

Write to us within 5 days of delivery with photos of the item and the box. We ship a replacement
right away. For items under $50 you keep or dispose of the damaged one; for items over $50 we send
a prepaid label and ask for the damaged item back.

### Why does my order need a signature?

Orders over $400 are shipped signature required so that a Sentinel or a Northstar is not left on
a porch. If no one will be home, you can redirect the package to a carrier pickup point from the
tracking page.

### Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

Call us or write before 2:00 PM Eastern on the day you ordered and we can change the address,
swap a color, or cancel the order outright. Once the warehouse has printed the label the order
cannot be changed; let it arrive and return it under the 40-day policy instead.

### What payment methods do you take?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay and Lantern Supply Co. gift cards. The
showrooms also take cash and personal checks drawn on a Maryland bank.

### Where do I find my order number?

It is on the confirmation email and on the packing slip, in the form LS-2026 followed by six
digits, for example LS-2026-004871. Since May 2026 you can also look an order up on the online
store with the order number and the email address you used, without an account.

## Returns and refunds

### Can I send something back if I changed my mind?

Yes. You have 40 days from the day your order was delivered to return any unused item in its
original packaging with all its accessories. Start a return on the online store, or call us, and
you will get an RA number to put on the box.

### Does the return period start when I order or when I receive it?

It starts on the delivery date recorded by the carrier, not the day you ordered or the day we
shipped. Since May 2026 the online store counts the 40 days from delivery automatically.

### Is there a restocking fee?

A 12 percent restocking fee applies to opened items that are not defective. There is no fee on an
exchange, on an item that arrived defective, or on an item that is still sealed.

### Who pays for return shipping?

You do, unless the item is defective or we sent the wrong thing. If you would rather not arrange
shipping yourself, we can email you a prepaid label for a flat $8.50, which we deduct from your
refund.

### How long until I see my refund?

Your refund goes back to the original payment method within 7 business days of the return
arriving at our Hagerstown warehouse. Your bank may take a few more days to post it.

### Can I return a lantern I have already put fuel in?

No. A fuel lantern or oil lamp that has had fuel in it cannot be returned, because we cannot ship
or resell it. If it is not working properly, it is covered by the warranty instead, and we will
repair or replace it.

### What can't be returned at all?

Clearance items marked Last Light, custom-engraved items, gift cards, opened fuel, and consumables
such as mantles and wicks are final sale.

### I got it as a holiday gift. Is the return period longer?

Yes. Any order delivered between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31.

### Can I return something at a showroom?

Yes, at either the Frederick or the Annapolis showroom. Bring the packing slip or the order number
and the showroom will process the return on the spot.

## Warranty and repairs

### How long is the warranty?

Every lantern and lamp is covered against manufacturing defects for 2 years from the purchase
date. The Sentinel 1000 and Sentinel 1000 Pro are covered for 5 years. The Heritage No. 7 brass
oil lamp is covered for 10 years under the Heritage Promise. Rechargeable batteries and Second
Light refurbished items are covered for 1 year.

### How do I make a warranty claim?

Email help@lanternsupply.example with your order number and photos of the problem. You will get a
claim number starting with WC- and a decision within 5 business days. If the claim is approved we
send you a prepaid label to our Repair Center in Hagerstown and either repair the lantern or
replace it.

### Does the warranty cover the globe or the mantles?

No. Globes, chimneys, mantles, wicks, bulbs and fuel caps are consumables and are not covered.
They are all sold as replacement parts.

### If I give a lantern as a gift, is it still under warranty?

Yes. The warranty transfers with a gift. It does not transfer if the lantern is resold.

### Can you fix a lantern that is out of warranty?

Yes. The Repair Center in Hagerstown services every current product and the Beacon 400. Drop the
lantern at either showroom or ship it to the Repair Center, and we will quote the repair before
doing any work.

## Products

### What is the difference between the Firefly 220 and the Firefly 220R?

They are the same size and give the same 220 lumens. The Firefly 220 ($34.99) runs on three AA
batteries, which are included, for 42 hours on low or 9 hours on high. The Firefly 220R ($54.99)
has a built-in 2,600 mAh rechargeable battery that charges over USB-C, runs 48 hours on low or 10
hours on high, and can charge a phone from its USB-A port. The 220R weighs 30 grams more.

### How long does the Firefly 220R take to charge?

About 3.5 hours from empty with a 5 volt 2 amp USB charger. Four blue lights beside the port show
how full the battery is.

### Is the Sentinel 1000 waterproof?

Yes. The Sentinel 1000 and the Sentinel 1000 Pro are rated IP67: dust tight and able to sit in a
meter of water for 30 minutes. They float. The Firefly lanterns are IPX4, which means splash
resistant, and the Beacon 600 is IPX5, rain resistant.

### Which lantern is brightest?

The Northstar Dual-Fuel, at about 1,500 lumens. Among the LED lanterns the Sentinel 1000 and
Sentinel 1000 Pro are brightest at 1,000 lumens.

### Does the Beacon 600 come with batteries?

No. The Beacon 600 takes four D cells, which are not included. The Firefly 220 is the only
battery lantern that ships with its batteries.

### Do you still sell the Beacon 400?

No. The Beacon 400 was discontinued in March 2025. We keep parts for it and service it until
March 2028.

### Do you price match?

Yes. If you find the same product at a lower price from an authorized retailer within 14 days of
your purchase, send us the link or receipt and we refund the difference.

### Can I use the Sentinel 1000 to charge my phone?

Yes. The Sentinel 1000 and the Sentinel 1000 Pro have a USB-A port that charges a phone, and the
10,000 mAh battery in the Sentinel 1000 will charge a typical phone about three times with the
lantern off. Charging a phone from the lantern shortens the lantern's own runtime accordingly.

### How do I replace a broken Beacon 600 globe?

Order globe G-600 for $14.99. Unscrew the top cap, lift the old globe off the posts, set the new
one in place and screw the cap back on; it takes about two minutes and no tools. Keep the rubber
gasket under the cap, as a new globe does not come with one.

### Do you sell refurbished lanterns?

Yes. Lanterns that come back and pass a bench inspection at the Repair Center are sold as Second
Light refurbished items at a discount, with a 1-year warranty. They are sold at both showrooms
and a few times a year online.

## Fuel

### Can I burn kerosene in the Northstar?

No. The Northstar Dual-Fuel burns Clearburn white gas or unleaded gasoline only. Kerosene or
lamp oil will clog its generator in a single evening and the damage is not covered by warranty.

### What fuel goes in the Heritage No. 7?

Clearburn lamp oil only. Kerosene will burn but smokes, smells, and voids the 10-year warranty.

### What fuel goes in the Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern?

K-1 kerosene or Clearburn lamp oil. Lamp oil burns cleaner indoors.

### How should I store fuel at home?

Keep it in the original container, away from heat, and no more than most Maryland counties allow,
which is 5 gallons. Sealed Clearburn white gas keeps for 5 years; once opened, use it within a
year. Never refuel a hot lantern; let it cool for 10 minutes first.

### Can I run a fuel lantern indoors?

The Hagerstown Kerosene Lantern and the Heritage No. 7 are made for indoor use in a room with
some ventilation. The Northstar Dual-Fuel is an outdoor lantern: it produces carbon monoxide and
must never be used in a tent, a camper or a closed room. The fuel safety guide has the details.

## Lumen Rewards

### How do I earn points?

Join Lumen Rewards for free and you earn 1 point for every dollar you spend, before shipping and
tax. Every 500 points is a $25 reward.

### Do points expire?

Points expire after 18 months without a purchase. Any purchase, in store or online, resets the
clock.

### What do the tiers get me?

Tiers are set by the points you earn in a calendar year. Ember is 0 to 999, Flame is 1,000 to
2,499, and Beacon is 2,500 and up. Flame members get free expedited shipping upgrades. Beacon
members get early access to Second Light sales and one free bench repair a year. Every member
gets a $10 birthday reward.
