# Shipping Policy and Operations

Last updated: September 28, 2026
Owner: Denise Okafor, Warehouse Manager

## Scope

This document covers how Lantern Supply Co. ships orders from the Hagerstown warehouse, what the
customer pays, how long delivery takes, what can and cannot be shipped where, and what to do when
a package goes wrong. Customer service agents quote from it; the online store's shipping page is
derived from it. Internal transfers between the warehouse and the showrooms are covered in the
last section.

## Rates and delivery times

All orders ship from the warehouse at 1180 Downsville Pike, Hagerstown, MD 21740. Delivery times
are in business days (Monday to Friday, excluding the six company holidays) and count from the
day the order leaves the warehouse, not the day it was placed.

| Service   | Cost                             | Delivery time         | Same-day cutoff |
|-----------|----------------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------|
| Standard  | $6.95, free on orders of $75 or more | 3 to 6 business days | 2:00 PM ET    |
| Expedited | $14.95                           | 2 business days       | 2:00 PM ET      |
| Overnight | $29.95                           | Next business day     | 1:00 PM ET      |
| Canada    | $19.95 flat                      | 5 to 10 business days | 2:00 PM ET      |

Standard shipping is free on orders with a merchandise subtotal of $75 or more, calculated after
discounts and before tax. Fuel and oversized items count toward the subtotal. Orders under $75
pay the flat $6.95. Expedited and overnight are never free, though Flame and Beacon tier Lumen
Rewards members receive free upgrades from standard to expedited under the terms of that program.

Overnight service is available only to addresses in the contiguous 48 states and only for orders
placed by 1:00 PM ET on a business day. An overnight order placed on a Friday before the cutoff is
delivered on Monday.

## Dispatch

Orders placed before 2:00 PM ET on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM
ET, on a weekend or on a holiday ship the next business day. The warehouse runs two pick waves, at
10:30 AM and 2:15 PM; the afternoon wave is the one that catches orders placed just before the
cutoff. Carriers collect at 4:30 PM.

Every order gets a tracking number by email when the label is printed. If the email has not
arrived by the next morning the order is almost always still in the warehouse; agents should check
the order status in Hearth before offering anything to the customer.

Orders that contain a backordered item are held until the whole order can ship, unless the
customer asks for a split shipment. A split shipment costs the customer nothing; the warehouse
absorbs the second label.

## Carriers

Standard orders ship by UPS Ground or USPS, chosen by the warehouse management system by weight
and destination. As a rule, packages under 1 lb go USPS and everything else goes UPS Ground.
Expedited orders ship UPS 2nd Day Air. Overnight orders ship UPS Next Day Air Saver. Canadian
orders ship UPS Standard to Canada.

The warehouse can ship by FedEx on request for a customer who cannot receive UPS, at the UPS
rate; this is set up by Denise Okafor's team on a per-order basis.

Carrier pickups are at 4:30 PM each business day. The UPS account number and the USPS permit are
held by the warehouse office; agents do not need them.

## Where we ship

Lantern Supply Co. ships to all 50 US states and to Canada. The company does not ship to any
other country, to US territories, or to APO/FPO addresses. Customers outside the United States
and Canada are referred to the authorized retailer list on the website.

### Canada

Canadian orders cost a flat $19.95 and take 5 to 10 business days. Duties, GST/HST and any
brokerage fee are paid by the recipient on delivery; the company does not collect them and cannot
estimate them. Fuel does not ship to Canada. Shipping to Canada was added with Hearth 4.2 on
August 11, 2026; before that, Canadian customers ordered by phone and paid actual carrier cost.

### Signature on delivery

Orders over $400 ship with a signature required. The threshold is applied to the merchandise
subtotal. Customers who cannot be present may redirect the package to a UPS Access Point through
the carrier's tracking page. The warehouse does not waive the signature requirement on request.

## Fuel and hazardous materials

Clearburn White Gas (LS-FUEL1), Clearburn Lamp Oil (LS-OIL1) and kerosene are limited-quantity
hazardous materials, shipped as ORM-D. The rules are:

- Fuel ships by ground only. It cannot go expedited or overnight; an order containing fuel is
  downgraded to standard shipping and the customer is told at checkout.
- Fuel does not ship to PO boxes.
- Fuel does not ship to Alaska, Hawaii or Canada.
- No more than 8 quarts of fuel ship in one package. Larger fuel orders are split.
- Fuel ships only in its original sealed container, in the warehouse's partitioned fuel cartons.

An order that mixes fuel with other items ships as two packages when the non-fuel items are
eligible for a faster service the customer has paid for. Otherwise the whole order ships ground.

Fuel-burning lanterns that have never had fuel in them are ordinary goods and ship by any
service. A fueled lantern, for example one coming back for repair, must be fully drained and
aired out before it ships; the Repair Center's instructions cover this.

Lithium-ion products (the Firefly 220R, Sentinel 1000 and Sentinel 1000 Pro) ship under the
carrier's battery-in-equipment rules and need no special handling by the customer. They do not
ship by USPS to Alaska or Hawaii; the warehouse uses UPS Ground for those destinations.

## Curbside pickup

Curbside pickup is available at the Frederick showroom only, at 412 East Patrick Street. It is
free and orders are ready within 2 hours of being placed during showroom hours (Monday to
Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET). An order placed after 4:00 PM is ready the next morning.
The customer receives a "ready for pickup" email, parks in the loading zone on East Patrick
Street and calls the showroom; staff bring the order out. Pickup orders are held for 7 days,
after which they are cancelled and refunded.

The Annapolis showroom does not offer curbside pickup; its stock is not connected to the online
store's inventory. Customers near Annapolis can order online and have the order shipped, or buy
from the showroom floor.

Fuel may be collected curbside, which is the simplest way for a Frederick customer to buy it.

## Lost, late and damaged packages

### Late

A package is late when it has not arrived by the last day of its delivery window. Agents should
first check the carrier's tracking; most late packages are moving and arrive within two business
days. For an expedited or overnight order that misses its window, the difference between the
service paid for and standard shipping is refunded without the customer asking.

### Lost

A package is treated as lost when 10 business days have passed since the expected delivery date
with no delivery scan. At that point customer service files a claim with the carrier and, without
waiting for the claim to settle, ships a replacement at no charge. If the original package later
turns up, the customer is asked to refuse it or to return it with a free prepaid label. Lost
package replacements over $500 need Tomas Reyes's approval under the Three Lights rule, like any
other credit of that size.

A package that tracking shows as delivered but that the customer did not receive is handled the
same way after a 3 business day wait, during which the customer checks with neighbors and the
building office. The carrier is asked for the GPS delivery scan.

### Damaged on arrival

Damage must be reported within 5 days of delivery, with photos of the item and the packaging. For
items under $50, a replacement ships at once and the customer keeps or discards the damaged item;
no return is needed. For items of $50 or more, a replacement ships at once and a free prepaid
label is sent for the damaged item, which the warehouse needs for the carrier claim. Damage
reported after 5 days is handled under the warranty policy where a defect can be shown, and
otherwise at the shift lead's discretion.

Glass globes and chimneys are the most common damage. The warehouse packs every item with a glass
part in a double-wall carton with the glass separately wrapped; a damaged globe reported with a
photo is replaced from parts stock (G-600, G-ND2, CH-7) without a full-unit replacement.

## Address problems

An order that comes back as undeliverable because of a bad address is refunded less the original
shipping charge, or reshipped at the customer's cost. The customer can correct an address in
Hearth until the order is picked; after that, changes go through the carrier and may add a day.

Packages addressed to a PO box ship by USPS only, which rules out fuel and lithium products to
Alaska and Hawaii.

## Packaging

Orders ship in plain brown cartons with the company logo stamped on one side. Gift orders can
include a handwritten note of up to 200 characters at no charge. The warehouse uses paper fill
and recycled corrugate; the only plastic in an outbound package is the carrier's label pouch and
the retail packaging the manufacturer supplied.

## Internal transfers

Stock moves between Hagerstown and the two showrooms on a company van every Tuesday. The van
leaves Hagerstown at 7:00 AM, reaches Frederick by 8:00 AM and Annapolis by 10:30 AM, and
returns with showroom returns and repair drop-offs. Transfer requests are entered in Hearth by
Friday noon to make the next Tuesday van. Urgent transfers can go by UPS Ground at the showroom
manager's discretion.

Repair drop-offs collected at the Annapolis showroom ride the Tuesday van to Hagerstown and are
logged at the Repair Center that afternoon.
